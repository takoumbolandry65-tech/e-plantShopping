Paradise Nursery – Online Plant Shopping Application

Paradise Nursery is a React-based online shopping application that allows customers to explore and purchase a variety of houseplants.

The application provides an easy-to-use shopping experience where customers can browse plants by category, view product information, and manage their shopping cart.

Features

Landing page introducing Paradise Nursery.

Product listing page featuring houseplants with images, descriptions, and prices.

Shopping cart functionality using Redux Toolkit.

Add plants to the shopping cart.

Increase or decrease plant quantities.

Remove plants from the cart.

Automatically calculate the total number of items and their cost.

Navigate between the Home, Plants, and Cart pages.

Checkout button displaying a Coming Soon message.
